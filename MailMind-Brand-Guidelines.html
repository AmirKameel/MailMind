MailMind
Brand Guidelines · v1.0

Inbox, with a mind of its own.

MailMind is an AI-first universal email client — Gmail, Office 365, Yahoo, AOL, IMAP — wrapped in one calm, adorable interface. These guidelines define how it should always look and feel.

01 Essence

Email is stressful. MailMind is the antidote — calm, intelligent, universal, and just a little bit adorable. Voice is friendly and lowercase-leaning. Short sentences. Plain words.

02 Logo

A rounded envelope with a gentle smile, and a soft spark above-right — the AI bit. 24×24 grid, 2px strokes, fully rounded joins.

07 Do & Don't

do
Lead with whitespace. Let the page breathe.
don't
Use blue for primary — that's every other email app.
do
Use serif sparingly — one moment of meaning per screen.
don't
Add calendar, tasks, contacts, or notes. Ever.
do
Mark every AI moment with the sparkle and --ai color.
don't
Use robot, brain, or wand icons for AI.
do
Default to soft shadows and rounded corners.
don't
Wrap everything in a card. Some things are just text.
